I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at the **University of Connecticut (UConn)**, advised by [Prof. Yuan Hong](https://yhongcs.github.io/). I joined UConn in January 2026.

My research focuses on **LLM security and reliability**, including jailbreak attacks and defenses, unified evaluation, and knowledge boundaries. I have also worked on cross-modal knowledge distillation and multimodal emotion recognition.

Previously, I received my B.Eng. degree in Computer Science and Technology from **South China University of Technology (SCUT)** in July 2025.
